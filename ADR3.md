# ADR 003 - Hardware Requirements

## Status - Proposed

## Context

MiniGameZ is being developed for Android devices. The team needs to determine whether the application requires access to specific device hardware, such as GPS, cameras, microphones, speakers, or biometric authentication.
## Decision
The application will not require specialized hardware features. Gameplay will rely on standard touchscreen interaction.
## Consequences
- Reduces the complexity of application development.
- Avoids unnecessary hardware permissions.
- Makes the application easier to test across compatible Android devices.
